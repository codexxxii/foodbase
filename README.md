# Foodbase

**Your kitchen, your cookbook.**

Foodbase is a personal cookbook web application designed to make collecting, organizing, and discovering your favorite recipes simple. Whether you're saving a family recipe or documenting something new, Foodbase gives you a dedicated space for everything you love to cook.

## Features

- **Create Recipes** — Add your own recipes with ingredients, instructions, images, preparation times, cooking times, and serving sizes.
- **Organize Recipes** — Categorize your recipes to keep your personal cookbook organized and easy to navigate.
- **Favorites** — Save your favorite recipes for quick and easy access.
- **Search** — Quickly find recipes within your personal collection.
- **Personal Cookbook** — Keep your recipes together in a dedicated personal space.
- **Responsive Design** — Access your cookbook from your desktop, tablet, or mobile device.
- **User Authentication** — Securely access and manage your personal recipe collection.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (Neon)
- **ORM:** Drizzle ORM
- **Authentication:** Clerk
- **Form Management:** React Hook Form
- **Validation:** Zod
